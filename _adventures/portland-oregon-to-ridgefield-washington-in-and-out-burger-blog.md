Portland Oregon to Ridgefield Washington In and Out Burger is a beautiful route that offers breathtaking views of the Pacific Northwest. This route passes through lush forests, rolling hills, and picturesque farmland, providing a serene escape from urban life.

## The Route

The main loop runs about 15.4 miles. You start at the lower parking area on North Pier 99 Street in Portland, follow the route north along I-5, pass through Vancouver, Washington, and come to an end at In and Out Burger in Ridgefield Junction, Ridgefield, Clark County, Washington, 98642.

## What You'll Find

The route passes through the historic town of Vancouver, Washington, with its charming shops and eateries. Along Pioneer Street (WA 501), you will see glimpses into the region's rich agricultural heritage.

## Notes on Filming

The light window in this area is decent. The views along I-5 are the most filmable — the scenery catches whatever light comes through.

## Getting There

From downtown Portland: north on I-5 to Ridgefield Junction, exit 16. In and Out Burger is located near the intersection of Pioneer Street (WA 501) and North 56th Place. No fee.
