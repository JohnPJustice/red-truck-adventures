The ARB Base Rack went on the truck after two seasons of trying to manage gear in the bed without a system. The problem wasn't space — the bed is large enough — it was access and organization. Camera cases sliding around, recovery gear buried under camping equipment, and no way to grab anything without unloading the entire bed first.

The rack solves the access problem by creating a separate storage layer. Camera cases go in the rack. Recovery straps and a high-lift jack go in the forward section. Camping gear fills the bed below. You can reach any of it without moving the rest.

## Build Quality

The ARB rack is Australian-made, designed for corrugated outback roads and wet coast forests with equal credibility. The welds are clean, the steel is heavy-gauge, and two years of Pacific Northwest weather — which means a lot of moisture — has produced no surface rust on the powder coat. The mounting legs are aluminum, which matters for corrosion resistance.

The load rating is meaningful. The rack has carried 180 lbs of gear on a sustained basis without any flex or noise at the mounting points. The dynamic rating means you can take rough roads without worrying about the joints.

## The Rail System

The ARB rail system runs the full perimeter and down the center. It accepts ARB's own accessories — light bars, hi-lift mounts, jerrycan holders — without drilling or modification. It also accepts most MAXTRAX-style accessories via the 1.5-inch rail standard.

The main accessories currently running: a Maxtrax mount on the rear cross-member (accessible without climbing), a small light bar on the forward rail for forest approach lighting, and four anchor points for ratchet straps when carrying longer items.

## Wind Noise

An empty rack at highway speed adds noticeable wind noise — a low tone that's audible but not intrusive. With a load on the rack, it changes and often reduces. Adding a wind deflector to the forward crossbar largely eliminates the tone. This is an easy fix and shouldn't be a deciding factor.

## Bottom Line

The ARB Base Rack is an organizational tool that also happens to be built for serious use. The price is real but the quality justifies it. If you're doing any kind of regular adventure work out of a truck — gear-heavy, multi-day, rough-road — this is the right solution. The organization gain is immediate and significant.
